Bô

FW26 · three pieces · two in stock

The first one we were willing to put the name on.

Bô is the current cycle: a Signature Zip-Up Sweater, a Wide-Leg Pleated Trouser and the Cocody Monogram Hat. All black, all limited production, all carrying the woven label with the blossom around the size.

It was photographed on a white seamless in the morning and filmed against canvas we painted ourselves in the afternoon. Both of those are in the collection as much as the clothes are.
