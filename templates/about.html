About us

Cocody is a district of Abidjan. We only put the name on something once it deserves to go back there.

Maison Cocody is a small label working between Amsterdam and Abidjan. It started with three samples nobody was supposed to see, worn for a year until they were worth selling.

We make one collection at a time, in black, and we do not move on until it is right. That is slower than it should be and it is the only way we know how to do it.

What we hold to

Few things, done fully

Three pieces in a cycle, not thirty. Every one has to earn its place on the rail, and anything that does not clear that bar stays a sample.

Black, on purpose

One colour means the cut has nowhere to hide. It also means everything we have ever made still works together.

The name is a standard

Cocody is a real place with real people in it. Putting the name on a garment is a claim, so we only make it when it holds.

The flower

On the woven label, the size you bought is the one the blossom holds. It is punched as metal eyelets on the caps, and it sits inside the M and C on the monogram.

It is a small thing that costs us money and that most people will never notice. It is on every garment anyway, and it is why picking a size on this site works the way it does.
