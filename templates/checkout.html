What just happened

Nobody looked up a rate
  1. Rate fetchedThe carrier was asked what this parcel costs to that address, using the weight and dimensions held against each piece. The price you saw was the price we pay.
  2. Payment clearedProduct and shipping taken together, in one charge.
  3. Label generatedProduced automatically and waiting in the fulfilment queue. No one opened a carrier website.
  4. Invoice sentEmailed to the customer within seconds, with the order confirmation.
  5. Left to doPack it, stick the label on, hand it over. That is the whole manual step.

Section 6 of the written document covers which carrier or aggregator does this, what it costs, and what has to be in place first — starting with real weights and box sizes on every product.
