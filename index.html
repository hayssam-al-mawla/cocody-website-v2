The signature feature

Brief 3.6 asks for one interaction that makes the site instantly recognisable, and suggests a flower. Maison Cocody already has one — and since 21 September it also takes the wordmark’s place in the masthead and turns as you scroll.

On the woven label inside every garment, the sizes run S · M · L · XL and the one you bought is the one a blossom sits around. The same flower is punched as metal eyelets on the caps and tucked inside the M and C of the monogram.

So the size picker on the product page does exactly that: choose a size and the blossom moves onto it. It is not decoration invented for a website — it is how the product already works, which is why it will not feel gimmicky in a year.

The blossom drawn here is the logo pack's own path, not a redrawing of it. One file does all of it — stroked as an outline, opened into a ring so a letter can sit in the middle, or filled solid — which is why it stays the same mark wherever it turns up.
