We hung two canvases, painted them over a weekend, and shot everything in a day. The newest film always sits at the top of the Lab, and new uploads land there on their own.

We only put the name on something once it deserves to go back there.

Cocody is a district of Abidjan. It is where the family is from, and it sets the standard: nothing leaves the studio unless it would hold up at home.

That is why a cycle is three pieces and not thirty, why they are all black, and why the size on the woven label sits inside a flower — a small thing, done properly, on every garment we make.
